## Review Focus

These are the five failure modes a user is most likely to hit that the spec implies but no feature test exercises, most likely first. Each is pinned by the named tests in its task.

1. **Holding ⏪ or ⏩ when something goes wrong.** The cases:
   - holding ⏪ into bar 1;
   - holding past the sequence's end;
   - releasing the mouse outside the button;
   - the window closing or the bar being torn down mid-hold (the mixer window closed);
   - recording starting mid-hold, so a wind is refused;
   - a ⇧-press held;
   - a VoiceOver or channel press.

   Expected:
   - ⏪ stops at 0;
   - a release anywhere ends the hold;
   - teardown cancels it with no further move and no timer left running;
   - a refusal stops the hold and says why once, in the status line;
   - a ⇧-press never winds and goes to the marker on release;
   - an accessibility press is a click.

   Pinned by `aHoldWindsFasterTheLongerItIsHeld`, `cancelStopsWithoutAMove`, `aRefusedWindStopsTheHoldAndSaysWhy` and `aShiftClickGoesToTheMarker` (B2), and `aHoldButtonPressesAndReleasesOnce` and `anAccessibilityPressIsAClick` (B3).
2. **Hostile steps and winds over the FFI and the channel.** The cases:
   - quarters `NaN`, `±inf`, `f64::MAX` and `-f64::MAX`;
   - a position at or near the 2⁵⁰ limit;
   - a pickup (negative) position;
   - a meter change between bars;
   - `transport.rewind` with params;
   - a misspelt id;
   - no sequence;
   - a text field holding the keyboard.

   Expected:
   - non-finite quarters are refused with "quarters must be finite";
   - everything else clamps to `0…WIND_LIMIT`;
   - a step lands on a barline of the meter in effect;
   - params are refused as "Rewind takes no parameters";
   - nothing moves while a text field has the keyboard.

   Pinned by `bar_step_moves_one_bar_across_meter_changes`, `bar_step_and_wind_target_clamp`, `hostile_steps_and_winds_are_refused_or_clamped` and `rewind_and_fast_forward_take_no_parameters` (B1), and `rewindAndFastForwardWaitAndRefuseAsTheOtherTransportKeys` (B2).
3. **Leaving a typed field without Return.** The cases:
   - clicking another control;
   - clicking the arrangement;
   - ⌃⌘↓ hiding a pane;
   - closing the window;
   - Esc;
   - Tab and ⇧Tab;
   - focus moving between the popover's Punch In and Punch Out;
   - pressing Record or closing the popover with a draft;
   - text equal to what the field shows.

   Expected:
   - Return, Tab and ⇧Tab commit, and so does moving between the popover's two punch fields (Ruling 7);
   - every other way out cancels: the field shows the value in use again and nothing reaches the core;
   - unchanged text sends nothing.

   Pinned by `clickingAwayCancelsATypedField`, `tabCommitsAndMovesOn`, `thePopoversPunchFieldsCancelWhenFocusLeavesThem` and `recordAndClosingThePopoverDropPunchDrafts` (B5), and the e2e's click-away block (B11).
4. **A narrow or crowded transport bar.** The cases:
   - the 700-pt minimum window;
   - the mixer window below 420 pt;
   - step entry's summary shown;
   - each of the six themes;
   - a long device name;
   - digits changing while playing.

   Expected:
   - the readouts and the fields wrap onto their own lines rather than cover the buttons, tabs or toggles;
   - each box keeps its width as digits change, so the bar does not jitter;
   - every box reads in every theme (≥ 4.5:1).

   Pinned by `theFlowWrapsReadoutsAboveTheFieldsWhenNarrow` and `theReadoutLookFollowsTheTheme` (B4), and README 225 by eye.

3. **Ruling (what ⏮ ⏪ ⏩ do; owner 2026-11-04, binding, with the controller's details).**
   - **⏮** runs `transport.return_to_start` (`DocumentModel.returnToStart()`), as the menu item does.
   - **A click on ⏪ or ⏩** moves one bar: `core_editor::transport::bar_step`.
     - Forward goes to the first barline after the position: `barline_at_or_after(tick + 1)`.
     - Back goes to the last barline before it: `barline_at_or_before(tick − 1)`, and 0 at or before 0.
     - Barlines follow the meter in effect.
   - **⇧-click** runs `transport.previous_marker` or `transport.next_marker`. With no marker that way, the status line says "No marker before the position." or "No marker after the position.", as today. A ⇧-press never winds: the jump happens on release.
   - **Holding** winds continuously, faster the longer it is held.
     - The rate is the surfaces' shuttle curve, `core_surface::shuttle`, so a keyboard controller's Rewind and VizDAW's button wind alike: nothing below 0.4 s, then 4, 8, 16 and 32 quarter notes a second from 0.4, 1.4, 2.4 and 3.4 s.
     - `TransportShuttle` ticks every `tickEvery` = 0.1 s. Each tick winds by `shuttle_quarters(last_ms, now_ms)`, negated for ⏪, through one `transport_wind`.
     - A release with no wind yet is a click: one bar, or the marker with ⇧. A release after winding adds the last part of the wind and no bar step.
   - **Playback keeps running** from the new place. A step or wind is a `locate`, as a ruler click is.
     - While an automation pass writes, each step lands the pass, as a ruler click would (plan 5e-1 Ruling 23). **(controller, provisional; owner may override)**
     - While recording, the core refuses with Busy "Stop recording first.". It shows in the status line, and the hold stops.
   - **Where a step starts.** It starts from the target of the last step, wind or surface skip if that was under 250 ms ago, else from the transport's position or the cursor. This uses the session's existing `surfaces.wind` memory (`WIND_RECENT`), so fast clicks and a running wind never stutter on a lagging poll.
   - **Limits.** The position is never below 0 nor above `core_editor::transport::WIND_LIMIT` = 2⁵⁰ (`TimeAxis.limit`).
   - The surfaces' own Rewind and Fast Forward are unchanged: a tap goes to the marker, else the bar line (plan 5f-1 Ruling 5).
   - Why: the owner's behaviour of 2026-11-04 and the real-time rule ("no render-thread work beyond setting the position").
   - Cost if wrong: the constants live in one Swift type and one Rust module.
4. **Ruling (keys) (controller, provisional; owner may override).**
   - `transport.rewind`, "Rewind", menu Transport, key `⇧,`, keypad twin `Keypad-`.
   - `transport.fast_forward`, "Fast Forward", menu Transport, key `⇧.`, keypad twin `Keypad+`.
   - **The choice.** Many DAWs use `,` and `.` for rewind and fast forward, and the keypad's − and + for the same. VizDAW's `,` and `.` already go to the markers. The shifted keys keep the markers where they are and sit beside them.
   - **Step entry.** `Keypad+` stays Tie during step entry, where step keys win over global ones, as `1` does over Locator 1.
   - **A held key** steps one bar per key repeat: both ids join `KeyDispatch.repeating`. Acceleration is the button's only, because the key path sees no key-up.
   - **Settings and the menu.** Both are rebindable in Settings › Keys and listed under Transport. Their menu items sit after Return to Start, and choosing one steps one bar.
