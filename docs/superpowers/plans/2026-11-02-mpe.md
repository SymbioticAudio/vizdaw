**Goal:** MPE controllers record and play as MIDI 2.0 per-note events, and per-note expression reaches MIDI 1.0 synths and plugins as MPE.
- **Input.** A lane's MIDI input can be set to MPE: lower or upper zone, member channels, per-note pitch-bend range. The setting is also taken from the controller's MPE Configuration Message. MPE turns a controller's channel-per-note MIDI 1.0 into per-note events before input effects, thru and recording: notes, per-note pitch bend, pressure, and CC74 timbre.
- **Output.** A destination can be set to MPE output. Its notes are then rotated across member channels, so per-note expression survives the down-conversion to MIDI 1.0, both in playback and in thru. Without it, that expression is dropped and counted.
- **Control and UI.** The settings are in the track header's input and destination menus and on the control channel.
- **Tests.** Golden tests and round trips (MPE in → per-note events → MPE out) prove it.

**Architecture:**
- **`core-model`** gains `core_model::mpe::{MpeConfig, MpeZone}`, used in two places:
  - `Lane.mpe` with `LaneCmd::SetMpe` (the input side, Ruling 3);
  - `Destination.mpe` with `RoutingCmd::SetMpe` (the output side).

  Both are saved only when set. `file::VERSION` goes 6 → 7 with an identity `migrate_v6_to_v7` (P3), so an older build says NewerFormat.
- **`core-engine`** gains `core_engine::mpe`:
  - the pitch-bend scaling (`bend_in`/`bend_out`) and the configuration bytes;
  - `MpeDecoder` (MIDI 1.0 → `LiveMsg`, with MCM and RPN 0 parsing);
  - `MpeEncoder` (`LiveMsg` → MIDI 1.0 on rotating member channels, stealing the oldest note).

  All three have no allocation, no lock and no I/O. The renderer decodes each MPE lane's input before plan 5g-1's chain, and encodes thru for MPE destinations in `LiveOut::settle`, from encoders kept in a parallel array on `InputRoutes` (P6). MCM changes go back to the control side through a lock-free report ring. The schedule compiler runs each MPE destination's events through the same encoder at compile time, which also covers 5f-2's offline bounce; its configuration is also in `Schedule.preamble`, resent on a chased start (P8). An MPE destination's channel-wide traffic (patch, CC 7/CC 10, `send_now`) goes on the zone's manager channel (P1), and its mute applies before encoding (P2).
- **`core-ffi`**:
  - `passes` turns recorded per-note messages into notes' `per_note` (bend, timbre #74) and `PolyPressure` events (pressure).
  - The FFI gains `MpeRow` and `MpeZoneRow`, `LaneRow.mpe`, `DestRow.mpe`, `LaneEdit::SetMpe` and `Session::set_destination_mpe`.
  - The session's idle writes a received MCM back as a lane edit.
  - `state.get input` gains the per-note drop and steal counts.
- **`core-control`, Swift, `vizctl`:**
  - The control channel gets `lane.set_mpe` and `destination.set_mpe`. `state.get document` adds an `mpe` key to each lane object and to each object of its existing `destinations` list (5g-1b).
  - The track header gets MPE sections in its input and destination menus. The input title reads "Port · MPE" everywhere `LaneInputs` titles it (P11), and the MIDI strip's kind line reads "MIDI · MPE Lower/Upper" (P5).
  - Also in this plan: the MPE e2e, README 251–256, and backlog rows.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 2024 (`core-model`, `core-engine`, `core-ffi` with UniFFI 0.32.2, `core-control`, `vizctl`), Swift 6 / SwiftPM (Swift Testing), macOS 14+.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-recording-audio-design.md`, §13 row "5g-2 — MPE input and output", split from the former 5g row by plan 5g-1 Ruling 1. This plan delivers the owner's MPE addition of 2026-10-20:

It also delivers the base spec's "MIDI 2.0 throughout … per-note controllers and per-note pitch bend" (`2026-10-15-vizdaw-design.md` §1, §5) for live input. MPE behaviour follows the public MIDI Polyphonic Expression specification:
- zones with a manager channel (1 or 16) and member channels;
- the MCM (RPN 6 on the manager channel, data entry = member count, 0 = zone off);
- pitch-bend sensitivity by RPN 0 (member default 48 semitones);
- the per-note controls: member pitch bend, channel pressure and CC 74.

## Review Focus

These are the five failure modes a user is most likely to hit that the spec implies but does not spell out, most likely first. Each is pinned by named tests in its task.

1. **More notes than member channels, and release tails.** A ten-finger chord into a 7-member zone. A note started on a channel whose previous note is still in its release.
   - Expected:
     - new notes take the free channel released longest ago, so a fresh note never bends a tail;
     - with none free, the oldest note is ended on its channel first, then reused, and the steal is counted;
     - nothing hangs.
   - Pinned by `notes_rotate_to_the_channel_released_longest_ago`, `the_oldest_note_is_stolen_and_counted` (Task 2), `playback_rotates_and_steals_like_thru` (Task 4) and `mpe_stolen_is_reported` (Task 6).
2. **Expression before the note, and expression on the wrong channel.** A controller sends bend, CC74 and pressure before its note-on, as MPE controllers do. A member channel gets bend with no note sounding. A manager-channel bend or sustain pedal.
   - Expected:
     - the pre-note values become the note's starting expression: recorded at offset 0, sent before the note-on on output;
     - a member bend with no note only sets the channel's next start;
     - manager messages are channel-wide and go to the output's manager channel.
   - Pinned by `expression_sent_before_a_note_is_its_start`, `manager_messages_are_channel_wide` (Task 2), `live_mpe_round_trips_to_an_mpe_destination` (Task 3) and `mpe_round_trips_through_a_recording` (Task 5).
3. **A controller that reconfigures itself.** An MCM shrinks the zone from 15 to 7 members, or turns it off (0). RPN 0 sets a 24-semitone bend range. The MCM arrives while recording.
   - Expected:
     - decoding follows at once;
     - the track's setting follows as one undo step "Set Track MPE", applied after the pass when it arrives during a recording;
     - a zone turned off makes the lane's input plain MIDI;
     - the RPN messages never play thru or record.
   - Pinned by `the_mcm_and_rpn_0_reconfigure_the_zone` (Task 2), `an_mcm_is_reported_and_keeps_the_route` (Task 3), `an_mcm_writes_the_lanes_setting_back` and `an_mcm_during_a_recording_waits_for_the_pass` (Task 6).
4. **Per-note expression into something that cannot take it.** An MPE lane playing a non-MPE destination. Bends past ±48 semitones. A recorded MPE part played on a plain synth.
   - Expected:
     - pressure becomes poly pressure;
     - per-note bend and timbre are dropped and counted (`per_note_dropped`; the compile report's dropped per-note count);
     - bends past ±48 are clamped at ±48;
     - nothing fails.
   - Pinned by `a_plain_destination_keeps_pressure_and_counts_the_rest` (Task 3), `bends_scale_by_range_and_clamp_at_48` (Task 2) and `a_plain_destination_drops_per_note_bend_and_reports_it` (Task 4).
5. **Hostile settings and bytes.** Members 0 or 16, a bend range of 0 or 97, an unknown zone, a 16-member zone, RPN data with no RPN selected, a running-status jumble, an MCM on a channel outside the zone.
   - Expected: refused with range errors (model, FFI, channel) and nothing changes; stray bytes are passed as plain MIDI or ignored, never panicking.
   - Pinned by `hostile_mpe_settings_are_refused` (Task 1), `hostile_mpe_bytes_are_ignored_or_passed` (Task 2), `hostile_mpe_calls_are_refused` (Task 6), `mpe_commands_check_their_params` and `mpeCommandsCheckTheirArguments` (Task 7).

## Plan Rulings

Each ruling decides something the spec leaves open, in the format "what — why — cost if wrong".

1. **Ruling (the split and the order):** this is plan 5g-2 of plan 5g-1's Ruling 1. MPE decoding runs before the input effects, on the path 5g-1 Ruling 2 orders:
   1. raw bytes: learned maps and a waiting learn take theirs first (5g-1 owner ruling; P12);
   2. lane match;
   3. **MPE decode**;
   4. input effects;
   5. merge;
   6. thru with **MPE encode** for MPE destinations, and the recorder.
   - Why: transpose and chord must move a note's expression with it, which needs per-note events. Learn sees the controller's raw bytes, and a message it takes is kept out of the lanes.
   - Cost if wrong (amended, P12): a CC 74 or pitch bend learned to a fader on the controller's port never reaches the MPE track, so its timbre or bend stops. README 252 and a backlog row say so.
2. **Ruling (`MpeConfig`):** `MpeConfig { zone: MpeZone, members: u8, bend_range: u8 }`. `MpeZone` is `Lower` (manager channel 1, members 2 upward) or `Upper` (manager channel 16, members 15 downward).
   - Members are 1–15 and the bend range is 1–96 semitones. The default is Lower, 15, 48.
   - The same type serves a lane's input and a destination's output.
   - A lane decodes one zone. A second lane can take the other zone of the same port. Channels outside the lane's zone are not that lane's.
   - The lane's own channel filter is ignored while MPE is on: the zone defines its channels.
   - Why:
     - one zone per lane matches how MPE controllers are used, and a dual-zone controller can feed two lanes;
     - the MPE defaults are a full lower zone and a 48-semitone member range.
   - Cost if wrong: a dual-zone controller into one lane needs two lanes.
3. **Ruling (where the input setting lives): `Lane.mpe`, beside `Lane.input`, not inside `LaneInput::Midi`.**
   - `LaneCmd::SetMpe { seq, lane, mpe: Option<MpeConfig> }` is named "Set Track MPE" (every `LaneCmd` label says Track since the Tracks plan; P13). It is allowed while recording, because it does not change which lanes record.
   - The setting is used only while the lane's input is MIDI.
   - Why:
     - "per-input" is per lane input, since a lane has one input;
     - a separate command keeps 5d-1 Ruling 18's "no input changes while recording" off it, which an MCM arriving mid-take needs;
     - it keeps `LaneInputRow` and its 13 Swift pattern matches unchanged.
   - Cost if wrong: one field moves into the enum, with a migration-free serde change.
4. **Ruling (the MCM and RPN 0, from the public MPE specification).** Per channel, the decoder tracks the RPN selected by CC 101 (MSB) and CC 100 (LSB).
   - CC 6 (data entry) with RPN 0/6 on the lane zone's manager channel is an MCM: members = min(value, 15), and 0 turns the zone off.
   - CC 6 with RPN 0/0 on a member channel sets the member pitch-bend range (1–96; 0 is ignored). On the manager channel it is consumed.
   - CC 101, 100, 6 and 38 on the zone's channels are consumed: never thru, never recorded.
   - An MCM for the other zone is ignored by this lane.
   - The decoder applies a change at once and reports it: `take_changed() -> Option<Option<MpeConfig>>`. The renderer pushes `(LaneId, Option<MpeConfig>)` to the report ring. `Engine::take_mpe_reports` drains it.
   - The session's `idle` writes a changed setting back with `LaneCmd::SetMpe` (Ruling 3), as an ordinary undo step. While this session records, finishes a pass or holds an open edit, the write waits (`Inner.mpe_owed`) and lands after.
   - `MidiRoute::carries` compares a new route's decoder setting with the old decoder's *live* setting, so the write-back does not restart the lane's chain.
   - Why:
     - the owner's "also set from the MPE Configuration Message";
     - writing it back keeps the UI, the file and the decoder in agreement;
     - waiting keeps one recording's undo step about the recording.
   - Cost if wrong: an undo step appears when a controller is plugged in, and the user may undo it.
5. **Ruling (per-note pitch bend scale): VizDAW's 32-bit per-note pitch bend is ±48 semitones full scale.** 48 semitones is the MIDI 2.0 per-note default and MPE's member default.
   - `bend_in(v14, range) = CENTER + round((v14 − 8192) × range × 2^18 / 48)`, clamped to `u32`.
   - `bend_out(v32, range) = 8192 + round((v32 − CENTER) × 48 / (range × 2^18))`, clamped to 0–16383.
   - With equal ranges in and out (≤ 48), every 14-bit value round-trips exactly. A bend past ±48 semitones clamps.
   - Why: one stored scale makes recorded bends independent of the controller's range, and a destination with another range gets the same pitch.
   - Cost if wrong: a 96-semitone controller loses its outer half, as a stored bend clamped at ±48.
6. **Ruling (what a decoded MPE note is), in plan 5g-1's `LiveMsg`:**
   - **The note.** A member note-on is `NoteOn { pitch, velocity: up7(v), expr }`, where `expr` is the channel's current bend, pressure and timbre (sent before the note, as MPE controllers do). Its note-off is `NoteOff`.
   - **Expression while sounding.** Member pitch bend becomes `PerNote { Bend, bend_in(…) }`, channel pressure `PerNote { Pressure, up32(v, 7) }`, and CC 74 `PerNote { Timbre, up32(v, 7) }`. Each goes to every note sounding on that channel; with none sounding, it only sets the channel's state. Member poly pressure goes to its pitch.
   - **Everything else.** Other member messages are `Channel` messages. Manager-channel messages decode as plain MIDI 1.0 (`LiveMsg::from_midi1`).
   - **Recording** (`passes`):
     - `NoteOn.expr` becomes the note's `per_note` entries at offset 0: `PitchBend` when bend ≠ `CENTER`, `Registered(74)` when timbre ≠ `CENTER`. A `PolyPressure` event at the note's tick holds pressure ≠ 0.
     - A later `PerNote { Bend | Timbre }` is attached to the latest sounding note of its pitch, at its tick offset.
     - `PerNote { Pressure }` is a `PolyPressure` event.
   - Why:
     - in MIDI 2.0, poly pressure *is* per-note pressure, and `EventBody::PolyPressure` already exists, plays on any synth and needs no model change;
     - MIDI 2.0's registered per-note controller #74 is sound controller 5 (brightness), MPE's timbre;
     - offsets keep expression with the note when it is edited.
   - Cost if wrong: pressure is not inside the note's `per_note`, so the piano roll's per-note lanes do not show it; the Strip Chart's poly-pressure row does.
7. **Ruling (`MpeEncoder`), for live thru and compile time alike:**
   - **A note-on:**
     1. take the free member channel released longest ago (never-used channels first, in member order);
     2. with none free, steal the one whose note started first: its note-off goes first, and the steal is counted;
     3. then send, on that channel, pitch bend `bend_out(expr.bend, range)`, CC 74 `value7(expr.timbre)` and channel pressure `value7(expr.pressure)`;
     4. then the note-on.
   - **While it sounds,** `PerNote` messages go to its channel: bend `E`, timbre `B 74`, pressure `D`. `PerNote` for a pitch not sounding is dropped.
   - **A note-off** frees its channel.
   - **`Channel` messages** go to the manager channel. So do the destination's default patch, its `midi_mix` CC 7/CC 10 and `Engine::send_now` (P1). Frame-0 order: configuration, patch, mix.
   - **The configuration** is `configuration(cfg) -> [Midi1Msg; 11]`: the MCM on the manager channel (RPN 0/6, data entry = members, then RPN null), then RPN 0/0 = the bend range on the first member channel (data entry, CC 38 = 0, RPN null). It goes out:
     - at frame 0 of every schedule that plays an MPE destination, class Patch, like a default patch;
     - on each thru target when a route with an MPE destination is installed and not carried.
   - **The destination's own channel** (a MIDI endpoint's `channel`) is ignored while MPE output is on.
   - **Mute** (5g-1b `midi_mix.mute`) drops `NoteOn`, `NoteOff` and `PerNote` before the encoder, live and at compile; `Channel` messages still play (P2).
   - Why: rotating to the channel released longest ago keeps release tails unbent; the MPE specification recommends the reset before the note.
   - Cost if wrong: a synth that is already in MPE mode receives a configuration it did not need.
8. **Ruling (two senders on one MPE synth).** Each route's thru has its own encoder, and playback has the compile-time encoder. A lane playing along live with a recorded MPE part on the same synth can put two notes on one member channel; the later one then bends both.
   - Why: a shared per-target encoder would need the renderer to merge the schedule's channel choices with live ones every block, for a rare case.
   - Cost if wrong: an occasional cross-bend when playing along with an MPE part on the same synth. A backlog row records it.
9. **Ruling (non-MPE destinations).**
   - **Live thru:** `PerNote { Pressure }` plays as poly pressure. `PerNote { Bend | Timbre }` is dropped and counted in `Counters.per_note_dropped`. `NoteOn.expr` is dropped without a count, since it is only the starting state.
   - **Compile:** non-MPE destinations behave as since 2b. `CompileReport.dropped_per_note` counts dropped per-note data, now including a recorded MPE part played on a plain synth.
   - **MPE destinations at compile:** every `PerNoteKind` other than `PitchBend` and `Registered(74)` is dropped and counted the same way.
   - Why: "instead of being dropped and counted" applies to MPE destinations; elsewhere the old rule stands.
   - Cost if wrong: none beyond the counts.
10. **Ruling (compile-time MPE).** For a destination with MPE output, the compiler does not translate each event alone. It collects the destination's flattened events as `LiveMsg`s:
    - each note is a `NoteOn` at its tick, with `expr` from its offset-0 `PitchBend`/`Registered(74)` entries and a `PolyPressure` of its pitch at its tick, and a `NoteOff` at its end;
    - each later `per_note` entry is a `PerNote` at `tick + offset` (inside the note);
    - each `PolyPressure` inside a note is a `PerNote { Pressure }`;
    - every other MIDI event goes through `translate` as now, onto the manager channel.

    It sorts them by (frame, class: off < patch < control < on, flattened order) and runs one `MpeEncoder` over them. Its output becomes `Pending` entries at those frames on the destination's targets. `CompileReport` gains `mpe_stolen`. Velocity-scale automation applies to the notes before encoding, as now.
    - Why: one encoder for live and compiled output guarantees the round trip, and 5f-2's offline bounce compiles the same schedule, so bounced MPE plugins get the same bytes.
    - Cost if wrong: compile time for very large MPE parts (one sort per destination).
11. **Ruling (the UI).**
    - **The input menu.** A MIDI lane's input menu gains an "MPE Input" section: Off, Lower Zone and Upper Zone, with the current one checked. While MPE is on, it also has "Member Channels" (1–15) and "Pitch Bend Range" (2, 12, 24, 48, 96) sections.
    - **The destination menu.** It gains "MPE Output: ‹destination›" with the same choices, setting the lane's destination (every lane playing it is affected).
    - **The header.** The input title reads "Port · MPE" (the "· Ch n" dropped) when MPE is on, computed in `LaneInputs.title(lane:)` so the track header, the strip input picker, the inspector and the accessibility label agree (P11). The MIDI strip's kind line reads "MIDI · MPE Lower" or "MIDI · MPE Upper" (P5).
    - **The model.** The choices come from `MpeMenus` in `VizDAWAppModel`, so they are tested without AppKit. Each choice is one undo step.
    - Why: §6.9's input picker is where the lane's input is set; the destination menu is the only per-destination menu on the track header.
    - Cost if wrong: a menu layout change.
12. **Ruling (the channel).**
    - `lane.set_mpe { seq, lane, zone: off|lower|upper, members?, bend_range? }`. Members default to 15 and the bend range to 48; `zone: off` refuses the other two.
    - `destination.set_mpe { dest, zone, members?, bend_range? }`, the same.
    - `state.get document` gains per-lane `"mpe": {"zone", "members", "bend_range"} | null`, and each object of its existing `"destinations"` list (5g-1b: `id, name, external, return_latency_ms, midi_volume, midi_pan, midi_mute`) gains `"mpe"` (P7). No new top-level key.
    - `state.get input` gains `"per_note_dropped"` and `"mpe_stolen"`.
    - Why: §12.1's style, with one command per setting.
    - Cost if wrong: two registry IDs to retire.

/// Plan 5g-2 (spec §13 5g-2, README 252): MPE injected into an armed MPE
/// track while recording lands as notes with per-note bend and timbre and
/// poly pressure; the lane and destination settings show in
/// `state.get document`; nothing was dropped. Offline device, no port.

#[ignore = "needs a window server and VIZDAW_E2E_APP"]
fn records_mpe_as_per_note_expression() {

    use core_model::event::{EventBody, PerNoteKind};
    use core_model::lane::LaneInput;
    use core_model::sequence::SeqLength;

    let mut app = App::start_with(&[]);

    let synth = b.dest("Synth", "builtin:synth", 0).unwrap();
    let (seq, keys) = b.sequence("MPE", "Keys", Some(synth)).unwrap();
    b.run(Command::Lane(LaneCmd::SetInput {
        seq, lane: keys, input: Some(LaneInput::Midi { port: Some("MPE Keys".into()), channel: None }),
    }))
    .unwrap();

    app.wait(&w, json!({"condition": "documents_at_least", "count": 1}));
    app.call("command.run", json!({"id": "lane.set_mpe", "params": {"seq": seq.0, "lane": keys.0, "zone": "lower"}, "window": w}));
    app.call("command.run", json!({"id": "destination.set_mpe", "params": {"dest": synth.0, "zone": "lower"}, "window": w}));
    let doc = app.call("state.get", json!({"target": "document", "window": w}));
    assert_eq!(lane_named(&mut app, &w, "Keys")["mpe"], json!({"zone": "lower", "members": 15, "bend_range": 48}));
    let dest = doc["destinations"].as_array().unwrap().iter().find(|d| d["name"] == "Synth").cloned();
    assert_eq!(dest.clone().map(|d| d["mpe"]["zone"].clone()), Some(json!("lower")), "{doc}");
    assert!(dest.is_some_and(|d| d.get("midi_volume").is_some()), "5g-1b's keys stay: {doc}");
    app.call("command.run", json!({"id": "transport.arm_previous", "window": w}));
    app.call("command.run", json!({"id": "record.set", "params": {"replace": true, "loop": false, "pre_roll": 0, "count_in": 0}, "window": w}));
    app.call("command.run", json!({"id": "transport.record", "window": w}));
    wait_for_record(&mut app, &w, |r| r["recording"] == true && r["frames"].as_u64() >= Some(24_000));

    wait_for_record(&mut app, &w, |r| r["frames"].as_u64() >= Some(48_000));
    inject(&mut app, "e1 10 60 d1 40 b1 4a 7f");
    wait_for_record(&mut app, &w, |r| r["frames"].as_u64() >= Some(72_000));

    wait_for_record(&mut app, &w, |r| r["recording"] == false);
    wait_for_undo(&mut app, &w, "Record");

     and timbre are there; pressure is in the poly pressure row. For an
     external synth, its MIDI strip reads "MIDI · MPE Lower" (or Upper),
     the meter moves with every note, and the fader and pan reach every
     note. A MIDI-learn map on the controller's pitch bend or CC 74 takes
     those messages first: the track then gets no bend or timbre from it.
253. Play the recording back to the MPE synth: it sounds as played, also
     when you start mid-song (the synth is told its MPE zone again). Play
     it to a plain synth: the notes play with poly pressure, and the

     MPE synth's strip: no notes play; controllers still do.
254. Plug the controller in (or send its MPE configuration) while the
     track is set to 15 members: if the controller says 7, the track's

255. Hold more notes than the destination's member channels: the oldest
